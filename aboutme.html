About the Project

The Missing Painting

Created by Zizhen Wu

About Me

Hi, I’m Zizhen.

I am a student who enjoys drawing, visual storytelling, and learning how websites work. I made this project because I wanted to combine a mystery game with a story about confidence and the pressure artists can feel.

This is one of my first larger story-game projects. I created the scenes, planned the choices, and used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to turn the idea into a playable webpage.

Story Concept

Why this story matters

The main character believes somebody stole her painting, but the mystery is really connected to memories she has tried to avoid. The dream lets her meet her younger self and understand that fear, not a thief, made the painting disappear.

The story does not say that one award fixes every problem. Instead, it shows that asking for support and choosing not to hide can be an important beginning.
