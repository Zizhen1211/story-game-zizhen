A Story Game

The Missing Painting

A story about art, memories, fear, and finding your own voice.

01 / Introduction

The night before the exhibition

You are a young painter preparing for your first city art exhibition. At sunset, you fall asleep at your desk. When you open your eyes, your finished painting is gone and the room has changed into a world made from your memories.

Explore six locations, collect two important items, and discover why the painting disappeared. Your final choice will decide the ending.

“The painting was hidden. Your voice does not have to be.”
